# Evaluation

Historical design-baseline metrics from a separately validated predecessor were:
20/20 execution success, 90% exact routing accuracy, 97.22% worker micro-F1,
100% dispatchable plans, and no timeout or generation failures in that small
development set.

These are **historical design baseline metrics**, not native-engine results and
not evidence of medical quality. The native engine has not been rerun on that
benchmark in this finalization work.
